Job Market Paper

Partial Identification in Models with Selectivity: Application to Texas Direct

When outcomes are only observed for people who selected into a choice — like students who complete a specific set of courses before transferring — standard corrections for this selection bias often require strong, hard-to-justify assumptions about how people make that choice. This paper develops an approach, built on random set theory, that instead works with the full range of possibilities consistent with the data. Applying the method to Texas's community college transfer policies, I find that completing transfer curricula reduces credit loss after transfer, but accumulating extra credits beyond what's required can lower the odds of completing a bachelor's degree.
