Yujin Kwon

PhD Candidate in Economics, The University of Texas at Austin

Econometrics Applied Microeconometrics Economics of Education

My research spans econometric theory, causal inference, and educational policy. I develop methodological tools to address the data challenges that arise in complex empirical settings and apply them to evaluate education policies in the United States, particularly in higher education. By integrating methodological innovation with empirical research, I aim to produce evidence that is both methodologically rigorous and policy relevant.
